Every blog starts with a post explaining why you're starting a blog. This is mine.

## Why write?

I've been building software for years, and I've learned most of what I know from other people's blogs, READMEs, and random posts on the internet. Writing is how I give some of that back.

There's also the selfish angle: writing forces you to understand things properly. If I can't explain something clearly, I probably don't understand it as well as I thought.

## What I'll write about

Mostly software engineering — distributed systems, web development, things I've built, things that broke, things I figured out the hard way. Occasionally other stuff if it seems worth sharing.

No promises on frequency. I'll write when I have something worth saying.

## The setup

This blog is built with **Next.js 15** and **Tailwind CSS v4**, with posts written in MDX. Engagement features (likes, views) are backed by MongoDB Atlas. The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/adithya-pulipaka/portfolio).
